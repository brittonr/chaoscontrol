## Why

ChaosControl retains four workload proof sets, but strict assertion identity blocks every set from current promotion. The project needs one fresh, complete KVM proof path before it expands product scope.

## What Changes

- Define one versioned proof cohort for strict schema-v2 assertion identity, KVM execution, snapshots, replay, artifacts, bounds, and non-claims.
- Produce and promote a fresh Raft proof first.
- Repeat the admitted path for Redb, network, and the downstream-shaped Rust workload.
- Make the Rust workload path reproducible from scaffold creation through promotion classification.
- Keep historical schema-v1 evidence diagnostic-only.

## Impact

- **Code**: replay evidence core, evidence shells, workload harness, and KVM dogfood runners.
- **Configuration**: typed Nickel proof cohort and retained-evidence contracts.
- **Evidence**: accepted workload manifests, replay verdicts, snapshots, receipts, and generated status documents.
- **Testing**: positive fresh-proof paths and negative stale, legacy, tampered, missing, and overclaim paths.

## Non-Goals

- No universal KVM determinism claim.
- No hosted, cross-machine, container, or non-Rust workload support.
- No migration that upgrades legacy assertion IDs into strict identities.
