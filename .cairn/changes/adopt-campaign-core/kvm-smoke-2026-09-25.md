## Harness incident (not product)

The first two attempts are retained under `/tmp/cc-kvm/p5/diag/` (pueue 260 and 261, legacy, at 128 and 256 MiB). Both stalled during guest boot after `Serial: 8250/16550 driver`, with the vCPU in `kvm_vcpu_block`.

The cause was the log timestamper. It decoded stdout as UTF-8 and died on the guest's `0xff` serial byte (the pueue log shows a `UnicodeDecodeError` traceback). The explorer's output pipe then stopped draining. After the timestamper was switched to raw bytes, the same boot reached `setup_complete` in about 5 seconds. No product code changed in this slice.

## Evidence class and non-claims

- Evidence class: each run is a bounded VM campaign. It is execution evidence only for the recorded kernel, initrd, binary, bounds, seed 42, and this host.
- The parity receipt, `frontier-trace.jsonl`, and the Campaign journal are structural decision evidence. They are not VM, fault, assertion, finding, replay, minimization, or release evidence.
- No replay, snapshot-backed reproduction, or minimization was attempted. The runs found no bug, so there is nothing to promote.
- Live observations (coverage, child moments, findings) are not claimed to agree between the policies. KVM repeatability is not established on this host (see the repeat runs). The spec scenario's "observations agree" is met only through replay of each run's recorded observations through the other policy. Receipt non-claim: live coverage drift is not compared.
- Uncovered paths: no explore draw occurred, and nothing was pruned. Seed 42 draws u = 0.6819, 0.9503, 0.4275, 0.6274, 0.2886 in rounds 0–4 of every run, all ≥ 0.1. These live runs did not exercise the explore-index mapping or `plan_pruning`; the P4 model corpus covers both.
- Only fault-schedule mode ran. Input-tree and hybrid modes, `--workers` > 1, resume, and journal recovery did not run under KVM.
- Publication durability is shown only by the live journal's committed chain and the checkpoint binding. Fault injection of fsync or rename did not run under KVM. The replayed Campaign backend in the checker writes a scratch journal without fsync.
- Time and memory are not measured. Throughput numbers in `report.txt` are incidental.
- `chaoscontrol-evidence::campaign_evidence_boundary` does not yet list `chaoscontrol.frontier-parity-receipt.v1` among the refused structural schemas (open from P4). The committed receipt is not submitted to any product admission gate.
